## The main economy

Most of us live and work in what we'll call the **main economy**: companies competing to sell, people competing for jobs, money flowing to whatever the market rewards.

It has done a lot. Over the last two centuries it has helped billions of people live longer, healthier and richer lives. Competition is its engine, and it is very good at producing wealth and new ideas.

But it isn't built to make room for everyone. It needs fewer people than want to take part, and it rewards some kinds of work far more than others. Many people are left out, badly served, or worn out keeping up. That isn't a flaw anyone designed. It's just not what the main economy is for.

## Our Economy

**Our Economy** is a second economy that we're starting to build next to it. Its engine is working together and looking after each other:

- **Everyone's in.** Anyone who wants to take part has a place, with whatever time they have (see [Any amount of time counts](/ideas/any-amount-of-time/)).
- **Everyone's an owner.** People own part of the companies and projects they work in, and share in a common fund that helps start new ones.
- **Built to snowball.** Every project and company helps start the next one, with people, tools and money.
- **Companies grow up together.** New companies start in groups that use each other's work (see [Practice companies](/ideas/practice-companies/)).
- **A gentler pace.** The aim is an economy where you can make a living without working all the time.

We're not the first to try this. Co-ops, mutual aid groups and community projects have done it for well over a century. We see ourselves as part of that family, with a proposal for how it could grow (see [Where we fit](/ideas/where-we-fit/)).

## Not instead, but alongside

We think the world works better with both, side by side:

- **The main economy** keeps doing what it does well: competing, innovating, producing wealth.
- **Our Economy** does what the main one isn't built for: making room for everyone, at a pace people can live with, with ownership shared by the people who do the work.

Nobody has to choose one forever. People and companies can take part in both, and change their mix over time (see [Your mix, your choice](/ideas/your-mix/)).

**We build; we don't take.** We're not trying to tear down, replace or take anything from what people have already built. We're building something new next to it, so the whole is more complete.
