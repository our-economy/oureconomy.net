## Site features
- **Comments on Ideas pages.** Wanted, to be more inclusive than Discord or GitHub. Requirements: commenting without an account (name only, held for approval at first), no ads or trackers, export of comments, free at our scale. Shortlist (checked 2026-11-24, confirm current terms before choosing): Hyvor Talk (free plan: 1 site, 200 comments, 200 pages), EchoThread (free Hobby tier for one site, around 10k page views and 1k comments a month), FastComments (no free tier, 30-day trial, exceptions for open-source projects), Cusdis (open source, no cookies, anonymous; hosted service status unclear, self-hosting needs a server). Adding one means a third party processes commenters' data: add a short privacy note and update `DECISIONS.md` ("no personal data collected").
